One Place for Everything NMR

openNMR is a free, open-source and vendor-agnostic app for working with your NMR data. No scripting needed. Just gorgeous figures and reliable analysis at the touch of a button.

Download Get started Browse tutorials 
pip install opennmr

Every tool in the toolbar has a guide

The buttons along the top of openNMR are the steps of a typical workflow. Pick one to see how it works.

  • Open

    Browse a folder of experiments and load one or several spectra.

  • Process

    Turn raw FIDs into spectra.

  • Compare

    View several spectra side by side.

  • Fit

    Fit your spectra reliably and accurately.

  • Integrate

    Quantify your spectra.

  • Simulate

    Synthesise spectra from your own parameters.

  • Dynamics

    Fit pseudo-2d data and explore the impact of mobility on spectra.

  • Advanced processing

    Specialised processing for more demanding data.

  • Apply

    Delve into advanced methods: eNMR, Imaging and more.

  • Periodic table

    Look up the properties of each nucleus.

Reads your data as it comes off the spectrometer

openNMR reads raw acquisition data, not a vendor's own processed output, so every spectrum is treated the same way whichever instrument it came from.

  • Bruker TopSpin
  • X-Pulse
  • Varian / Agilent
  • JEOL Delta
  • Magritek Spinsolve
  • SIMPSON

What’s new in 0.10 and 0.11

  • Fits and simulations in the background, with a queue and a progress window (Ctrl+R), so you can keep working while they run (0.11.0).
  • Open experiments as a tree: each spectrum's fits listed beneath it, with a table of every fit's parameters and errors to copy, export to CSV or restore (0.11.0).
  • A folder browser that scans in the background, remembers what it found and does not download online-only cloud files to list them (0.11.0).
  • A choice of fitting algorithm, including global searches, units in Compare, a pulse calculator, and a blank fit for each new dataset (0.10.2).
  • An aMAT spectral filter for weak signals hidden by intense ones at fast MAS, ORCA's orca_pnmr files and .xyz structures in DFT tensors, and vector figures that stay inside their axes in Illustrator (0.10.1).
  • Multiplets and J couplings found and named for you (d, dd, sept …), with coupling trees and the line a paper quotes.
  • A search box beside the toolbar, autosave with crash recovery, and Stop and Keep Best for a fit that is taking too long.
  • Search & Help functions. You can now search for shortcuts and for functions inside the GUI.
