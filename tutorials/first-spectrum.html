Before you start

You need openNMR running (see Install openNMR) and a folder of experiments from your spectrometer. openNMR reads the raw acquisition data directly: Bruker, X-Pulse, Varian/Agilent, JEOL Delta and Magritek Spinsolve. See Supported data.

Load a spectrum

  1. Start openNMR with opennmr-gui. The Open tool is selected, highlighted blue in the toolbar.
  2. Under Browse a folder, click Choose Folder… and select the folder that contains your experiments. The experiments found there are listed, with a count at the bottom.
  3. Double-click an experiment to load it. (To load several, Ctrl-click or Shift-click them and press Open Selected.)
  4. The experiment appears under Open experiments and its data is drawn in the viewer.

Look around

The tabs above the plot switch between the FID, the Spectrum and Spectral Fit. The buttons above the plot move you around it:

Zoom
Drag a box around the region you want.
Pan
Drag the spectrum around.
Home
Show the whole spectrum again.
Crosshair
Read off the position of the cursor.

If what you loaded is a raw FID, the next step is Process. It turns the FID into a spectrum you can phase, baseline-correct and reference.

Where next
