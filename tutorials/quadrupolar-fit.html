This tutorial is in preparation. It needs a good example dataset, which we have not settled on yet.

What it will cover

  • Fitting a half-integer quadrupolar nucleus (the central transition, with or without satellites) in Fit ▸ CSA, Quad & J-coupling or Quadrupolar (Czjzek distribution), for sites that have a distribution of quadrupolar parameters.
  • Starting values with Estimate from data, and why second-order perturbation theory is enough only while νQ ≪ ν0.
  • The two-step strategy that gives trustworthy numbers: fit the quadrupolar parameters (CQ, ηQ, δiso) from MAS data, then fix them and fit any CSA on a static spectrum.
  • How to tell a good fit from a plausible one: parameter correlations, fits at two fields, and the app’s own warnings.

Looking for a dataset

A good example is a half-integer nucleus (27Al, 23Na, 11B, 17O …) with a clear second-order lineshape, ideally recorded under MAS and also static, or at two fields, so the fit can be checked. If you have one you are happy to share, please open an issue.
